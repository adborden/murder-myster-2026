The gardener is tending the garden, picking hibiscus for Mr Blackwood’s
nightcap. The chauffeur is wandering the garden and leans down to sniff a red
flower. The gardener says to him, “Careful! That's red oleandar. We have lots
of poisonous plants in the garden.”
