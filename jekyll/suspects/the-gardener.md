name: The Gardener

Mr Blackwood had promised to invest funds in the gardener’s new
plant nursery. When Mr Blackwood pulled out of the deal, the
gardener was left with tremendous debt. The land was settled, the
construction had started, and the plants and seeds were already
arranged. With Blackwood out of the way, could the contract be
forged and the gardener’s dream be realized?
