# Whodunit?

{% responsive_image path: assets/img/the-solution.png %}

It was the chauffeur!

After speaking to the gardener in the afternoon, the chauffeur waits until the
gardener has finished their work. Once alone, the chauffeur returns to the
garden, picks some red oleandar, and leaves it with the hibiscus in the
kitchen. The chauffeur forgot to clean his shoes, tracking mud from the garden
back to his room. The maid unknowingly makes Mr. Blackwood’s nightcap with both
the hibiscus and the red oleandar, creating a poisoned concoction. Upon
ingesting the evening cocktail, Mr. Blackwood drops dead from the poison.
