# Whodunit?

It was the chauffeur!

After speaking to the gardener in the afternoon, the chauffeur waits until the
gardener has finished their work. Once alone, the chauffeur returns to the
garden, picks some belladonna, and leaves it with the lavender in the kitchen.
The maid unknowingly makes Mr. Blackwood’s nightcap with both the lavender and
the belladonna, creating a poisoned concoction. Upon ingesting the evening
drink, he drops dead from the poison.
